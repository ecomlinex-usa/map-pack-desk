A cadence for job photos and Google posts from material you supply. We do not invent a van, a crew, or a job that did not happen.

The listing stays on your Google account.

Map Pack Desk does not take owner access, does not transfer the profile, and does not hold the listing as leverage. You invite a manager. You can revoke that manager. Photos, reviews, and the business identity remain yours.
