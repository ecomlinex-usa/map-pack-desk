Refund Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026.

1. The offer

Setup is $497. The desk is $297 per month. The minimum paid term is 90 days. Billing is by invoice. This website does not collect cards.

2. Before we accept the shop

If you submitted a request or invoice intent and we decline the shop — wrong trade, outside US/Canada, or no owner control — we will not invoice you. If an invoice was issued in error and you have not paid, ignore it and tell us. If you paid an invoice before we declined, we refund that payment in full.

3. Setup fee after work starts

Once you have granted manager access (or refused to grant it after we invoiced) and setup work has begun, the $497 setup fee is not refundable. Setup includes audit, hygiene, and the first cadence plan.

4. Monthly fees and the 90-day minimum

Monthly fees cover the desk month on the invoice, not a rank outcome. During the 90-day minimum, unused days are not prorated and not refunded if you stop using the seat, revoke access, or close the shop. After the minimum, you may cancel with 14 days’ notice; we do not refund a month already in progress unless required by law.

5. Chargebacks

If you believe an invoice was paid in error, email desk@maps.codlinex.com before filing a dispute with your bank. We will not keep a listing hostage: you can revoke manager access regardless of a billing argument.

6. No result-based refunds

We do not refund because the listing did not rank, because calls were slow, or because reviews did not arrive. Those are not deliverables of this desk.
